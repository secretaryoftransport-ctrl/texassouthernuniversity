About the University

Learn about the institution, its purpose, and its commitment to higher education.

Who We Are

A place for learning,
leadership, and service.

Texas Southern University is a higher education institution located in Houston, Texas. This website presents university information, leadership resources, academic areas, student opportunities, and institutional updates in one central location.

The university experience is shaped by the pursuit of knowledge, a commitment to personal development, and the preparation of students to contribute to their professions and communities.

Our Story

University History

Recognizing the history and continuing development of the university.

A Continuing Legacy

Rooted in history.
Focused on the future.

Texas Southern University's history is an important part of its institutional identity. Its development reflects the continuing pursuit of educational access, academic growth, and opportunity in higher education.

This page is intended to serve as a central place for university historical information, milestones, and institutional achievements. Official historical dates and milestones should be verified before publication.

Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

The principles and aspirations that guide the university community.

Our Mission

Purpose in every pursuit.

To provide an educational environment that promotes academic achievement, develops leadership, encourages innovation, and prepares students for meaningful contributions to society.

Our Vision

Preparing leaders for tomorrow.

To be a recognized center of academic excellence, opportunity, discovery, and service, empowering individuals to shape the future of their communities and professions.
